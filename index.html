One raster, two jobs. The density layer is also the calculator. Its lossless WebP pyramid sits in population.pmtiles beside this page and is read directly with HTTP Range requests.

Population is encoded in 8-bit log-scale grayscale: black is zero and brighter pixels represent more people. The lasso chooses the finest zoom that covers its bounds with at most 16 tiles.

Pixels fully inside count fully; edge pixels count by the area overlapped. Interior tiles use exact precomputed totals, so only edge tiles need to be fetched and decoded.

Source: 2020 Census PL 94-171 block population (POP100), spread uniformly across true TIGER/Line block geometries. At z11, every block is rasterized across its polygon with supersampled pixel coverage; lower zooms sum their four children. The 8-bit log scale and calculation zoom automatically match the selection. Coverage includes the 50 states, DC, and Puerto Rico. Basemap: OpenFreeMap, rendered with MapLibre GL JS.
